Version 3.22.0: Crisis drops its timer and practice mode, and parties remember each mode

3.22.0 is a menus-and-modes patch. Combat rules are exactly the same as in 3.21.0, and the data that came with it has no new kioku and no balance changes. What changed is how Crisis runs and how the game remembers your teams.

In short

  • Crisis: no more battle time limit, practice mode is gone, and the skip button now shows whether you have the stamina for it.
  • Teams: the game remembers the last team you used per mode and per boss, saved with your account.
  • Battles: no rule changes. Every damage, buff and AI rule works as in 3.21.0.
  • Smaller things: featured characters on banners, mission reward guides, idle-reward screen, shop resets.
Mode change

Crisis

live in 3.22.0
  • No battle time limit. The countdown on the stage screen and in battle is gone, along with the "time almost up" warnings.
  • Practice mode was removed. A one-time tutorial pop-up tells you about it the first time you open Crisis.
  • Skip: the button now also shows whether you have enough stamina to skip.
  • The Crisis top screen can show a "ready to battle" badge.

Without a timer you can take your time with manual turns, for example to line up a Break before the big ultimates.

Quality of life

Teams remembered per mode

The game used to remember one last-used team per battle type. It now keeps the last team per mode and per target, for example per Crisis boss or per Mirror Tower. That choice is stored with your account, so it carries over when you move your data to another device. Entries for Score Attacks that have ended are dropped.

Wording

Retire warning

The warning before retiring from a high-score battle used to say your attempt would not be refunded. It now says the battle counts as a defeat and your high score will not update.

Also in 3.22.0

Summons

Banners can now mark some rate-up characters as the main pick-up, separate from the other featured ones. Some characters can get their own special summon animation.

Missions

Missions can be flagged for the reward guide (the new data flags many of them), and links in the game can now jump straight to one mission or bingo card and open its pop-up.

Events

Story events now have a display priority, which decides the order they are shown in.

Idle rewards

Player EXP shows as an item icon in the rewards pop-up instead of a separate text line.

Shop

Monthly-reset shops show their own countdown, and some shop lines can be set to skip the monthly reset.

Simulator status

The simulator now runs on version 3.22.0. The battle code did not change, so every result stays the same as on 3.21.0.
