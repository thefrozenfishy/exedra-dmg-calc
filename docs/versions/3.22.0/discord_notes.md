**Client update - 3.22.0**
What the new game version changes in how battles and menus work. Data changes are in the data-update notes.

**⚔️ Battles**
- No rule changes this time. Damage, buffs, conditions and auto-battle work exactly as in 3.21.0.

**🐉 Crisis**
- No more battle time limit: the countdown and the "time almost up" warnings are gone.
- Practice mode was removed. A one-time pop-up explains this when you next open Crisis.
- The Skip button now also shows whether you have enough stamina to skip.
- The Crisis top screen can show a "ready to battle" badge.

**👥 Teams**
- The last team you used is now remembered per mode and per target (for example per Crisis boss or Mirror Tower), not just per battle type.
- This is saved with your account, so it carries over with a data transfer. Entries for ended Score Attacks are dropped.

**⚠️ Retire warning**
- Retiring from a high-score battle now warns that the battle counts as a defeat and your high score will not update (it used to say the attempt is not refunded).

**🛠️ Menus**
- Summons: banners can mark some rate-up characters as the main pick-up, and some characters can get their own summon animation.
- Missions: missions can be flagged for the reward guide, and in-game links can jump straight to one mission or bingo card.
- Story events have a display priority that sets their order.
- Idle rewards: player EXP shows as an item icon.
- Shop: monthly-reset shops show their own countdown, and some shop lines can skip the monthly reset.

**🧮 Simulator**
exedra-dmg-calc runs on 3.22.0. The battle code is unchanged, so results are the same as on 3.21.0.
