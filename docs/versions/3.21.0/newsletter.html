Version 3.21.0: Venusta Dissonanza brings Step-Up skills and HP costs

Version 3.21.0 added two new combat mechanics, and the 16 October data shows who uses them first: Venusta Dissonanza, a 5★ Void Attacker Sayaka. Alongside her come a Sayaka (Anomalia) Score Attack, a new Link Raid and Halloween reruns.

In short

  • New kioku: Venusta Dissonanza (Sayaka Miki), 5★ Void Attacker, out 16 Oct 12:00 JST.
  • Step-Up skills: her Battle Skill can be powered up twice by spending extra SP before you use it.
  • Skills that cost your own HP: her skills drain her own HP but can never knock her out, and paying HP powers her up.
  • Events: Score Attack: Sayaka Miki (Anomalia) (16–30 Oct), Link Raid: Dessert Witch, two Halloween reruns, Mirror Layers 116–120, Exedra Quest 1-6.
  • Balance: the Mirror Layer 111–115 bosses now speed up only when they actually take damage.
New kioku

Venusta Dissonanza

5★ Void AttackerSayaka Mikiout 16 Oct
  • Battle Skill: Pesante Risonanza (1 SP). 90% void DMG to all enemies. Adds 1 Cadence Echo and one stack of +2% ultimate DMG (max 15), and costs 5% of her max HP. It opens her Void Field (all allies +30% void DMG and +30% void crit rate), or adds 2 stock to it if it is already up.
  • Step 1: Clamorosa Risonanza (2 SP). 200% void DMG to all enemies, 2 Cadence Echo, 2 ultimate-DMG stacks, costs 15% max HP.
  • Step 2: Clamorosa Risonanza + (3 SP). 220% void DMG to all enemies plus 5 random 10% hits, 3 Cadence Echo, 5 ultimate-DMG stacks, +30% SPD for 1 turn, costs 30% max HP.
  • Cadence Echo (max 4): +80% follow-up DMG. At 4 stacks she makes a follow-up attack.
  • Ultimate: 100% void DMG to all enemies plus 10 random 10% hits. Her Battle Skill DMG goes up for 2 turns, and she heals based on her ATK.
  • Ability: each time she pays HP, +2% ATK and +2% SPD (each max 10 stacks). The lower her HP, the less DMG she takes: −20% at 70% HP or more, up to −50% below 11%. Void hits by her team give her DMG stacks and MP.
  • EX crys: +10% ATK and +10% HP recovery; on follow-up attacks +2% SPD (max 10 stacks).

How she plays: Step 2 is her big turn. It costs 3 SP and 30% HP, but it fills Cadence Echo almost at once and stacks ultimate DMG five at a time. Because her damage reduction grows as her HP drops, she is built to run low on HP. Her own ultimate heal, plus a healer, lets her keep paying.

New mechanic

Step-Up skills

live with Venusta

A unit with a Step-Up skill gets a new button next to its Battle Skill. Each press raises the skill one step and swaps it for a stronger version. Cost: that version's own SP plus 1 SP per step. Venusta's step skills cost 1 SP each, so Step 1 costs 2 SP and Step 2 costs 3 SP. The button greys out when the team doesn't have the SP for the next step or the skill is at its top step. Cancel takes you back to the normal skill before you commit.

What auto battle does

On auto, the unit uses the highest step the team can afford, but only when its role's trigger is met. Otherwise it uses the normal skill:

  • Attacker (Venusta): an enemy is in Break.
  • Breaker: an enemy is not in Break yet.
  • Healer: an ally is below 50% HP.
  • Buffer: one of its own buffs on an ally has 1 turn left.
  • Debuffer: one of its own debuffs on an enemy has 1 turn left.
  • Defender: an ally has no barrier.

On auto, Venusta only steps up while an enemy is broken. If you want Step 2 outside Break, play her manually.

New mechanic

Skills that cost HP

live with Venusta

A skill can now spend the user's own HP: a percentage of max HP, a flat amount, or both, rounded up. It shows as its own HP-loss number in battle. The cost never takes a unit below 1 HP, so it can't knock out your own unit. At 1 HP the skill still works and costs nothing.

  • Passives can trigger on "paid HP this action". That is how Venusta's ATK and SPD stacks work.
  • In Link Raid, HP that a boss spends on its own skills counts toward the raid's total damage.
Events and stages

What to play

Score Attack: Sayaka Miki (Anomalia)

16–30 Oct JST, 50 ranks. The boss acts twice on even turns and three times on odd turns. Its Magic rises as its HP falls, and at full Magic it moves its own turn up. It takes much more damage once its break bonus reaches 600%, so plan around breaking it. It also has its own defeat cutscene.

Link Raid: Dessert Witch

20 levels. The next Link Raid season runs 22 Oct–5 Nov JST. The new EX Bonus for featured characters is still unused.

[Redux] Forever Halloween Night Part I and II

15 battles each, with three new portraits:

  • Where the Story Begins: +12.5% DMG on void attacks.
  • A Really Happy Halloween: +10% debuff hit rate; on a Debuffer also +10% SPD.
  • A Gift of Halloween Art: +12.5% ailment RES; on an Attacker also +12.5% ongoing DMG.

Balance

Changed rows

  • Mirror Layer 111–115 bosses: the action-order advance on your Battle Skills and ultimates now needs them to actually take damage. Hits they block or take no damage from no longer speed them up.
  • Mirror Layer counterattack (the forest DMG [XL] Magic attack) now counts as a follow-up attack instead of a Battle Skill. Effects that react to enemy Battle Skills no longer fire on it.
  • Text fix: one dark RES DOWN skill said "light RES", and the effect itself was already dark. A wording fix on Blast Haste.
Also in 3.21.0

Smaller changes

  • Link Raid shared HP: bosses can have several parts on one HP bar. Damage to any part counts toward your total, and when the bar empties, every part falls.
  • Magic Seal (no users yet): auto battle and enemies will aim it only at units it can land on.
  • Shop: bulk exchange, a Recommended exchange tab, and a reminder for unclaimed free Web Shop items.
  • Profile: shows your highest cleared Mirror Tower floor. Characters can now have alternate voice lines per costume.

Simulator status

The simulator runs on version 3.21.0 with the 16 Oct data. Venusta works in the PvE and PvP simulators. On auto she steps up by the game's rules, and in manual mode the action menu lists each Step-Up option with its SP cost. HP costs, the new "paid HP" condition and shared-HP Link Raid bosses are handled as in the game.
