Version 3.21.0: Step-Up skills and HP-cost skills are coming

This update adds two new combat mechanics to the game. No kioku uses them yet, so your current teams play exactly as before. Expect the first units with these mechanics in an upcoming banner.

In short

  • Step-Up skills: some Battle Skills can be powered up by spending extra SP before you use them.
  • Skills that cost your own HP: a skill can drain a share of the user's HP, but it can never knock them out.
  • Link Raid: support for bosses whose parts share one HP bar, plus an "EX Bonus" for featured characters.
  • Auto battle will aim Magic Seal only at units it can affect.
  • No new kiokus, events or stages, and no balance changes to existing skills.
New mechanic

Step-Up skills

no units yet

A unit with a Step-Up skill gets a new button next to its Battle Skill. Each press raises the skill one step and swaps it for a stronger version. Cost: that version's own SP plus 1 SP per step, so Step 2 of a 3 SP skill costs 5 SP. The button greys out when the team doesn't have the SP for the next step or the skill is at its top step. Cancel takes you back to the normal skill before you commit.

A passive on the kioku grants the Step-Up, so it belongs to that unit's own kit.

What auto battle does

On auto, the unit uses the highest step the team can afford, but only when its role's trigger is met:

  • Attacker: an enemy is in Break.
  • Breaker: an enemy is not in Break yet.
  • Healer: an ally is below 50% HP.
  • Buffer: one of its own buffs on an ally has 1 turn left.
  • Debuffer: one of its own debuffs on an enemy has 1 turn left.
  • Defender: an ally has no barrier.

What to watch for: a Step-Up unit drains team SP quickly. Expect them to pair well with SP-generating supports, and expect step levels to show up in skill descriptions ("at Step 2: …").

New mechanic

Skills that cost HP

no units yet

A new effect lets a skill spend the user's own HP: a percentage of max HP, a flat amount, or both, rounded up. It shows as its own HP-loss number in battle. The cost never takes a unit below 1 HP, so it can't knock out your own unit. At 1 HP the skill still works and costs nothing.

  • Passives can now check whether a unit just paid HP. Expect kits that reward it, such as "after consuming HP, gain…".
  • In Link Raid, HP that a boss spends on its own skills counts toward the raid's total damage.

What to watch for: heal-over-time and barrier supports will matter more for units that keep paying HP, and so will effects that scale with missing HP.

Link Raid

Shared-HP bosses and EX Bonus

not active yet
  • Shared HP: Link Raid bosses can now have several parts on one HP bar. When the raid's HP is synced with other players, every part updates. Damage to any part counts toward your total, and when the bar empties, every part falls.
  • EX Bonus: each Link Raid season can name featured characters who get an extra passive in that raid, shown on a new "EX Bonus" popup. No current season uses it.
Auto battle

Smarter Magic Seal targeting

no units yet

Magic Seal (it locks a unit's ultimate) was added in 3.19 and still has no users. When it arrives, auto battle and enemies will aim it only at units it can land on: units that are not sealed yet, have an EP gauge and don't use a BP gauge. Until now the targeting could pick a BP-gauge unit or one that was already sealed.

Everything else

Quality of life

Shop

Bulk exchange lets you buy several items at once. A new Recommended exchange tab can be tuned to your priorities. If you haven't claimed a free Web Shop item, you now get a reminder, with a "don't show again" option.

Profile

Profiles show your highest cleared Mirror Tower floor.

Battles and story

Some bosses get a special defeat cutscene; the first one is tied to a Sayaka boss. Characters can have alternate voice lines per costume, though none are set yet. Story chapters show unlock animations and better "new" badges.

Simulator status

The simulator now runs on version 3.21.0. It already supports Step-Up skills (including the auto-battle rules), HP-cost skills, shared-HP Link Raid bosses and the new Magic Seal targeting. It will handle the first units with these mechanics as soon as their data comes out.
