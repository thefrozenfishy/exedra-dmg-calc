Version 3.25.0: scratch cards, a new shop and Kioku ticket exchange

3.25.0 is a menus-and-events patch. Combat rules are exactly the same as in 3.24.0. The new client brings a scratch-card event, a rebuilt shop, a ticket exchange for home-screen cosmetics, bonus picks on wish-list banners and a new filter system. Several of these wait for a start time set by the server.

In short

  • Battles: no rule changes. Damage, buffs, conditions and auto-battle work as in 3.24.0.
  • Scratch cards: a new event type with cards to scratch, line rewards and missions.
  • Shop: new tabs for recommended items, sales, Magia Stones and subscriptions.
  • Kioku tickets: exchange them for home backgrounds, music and character models.
  • Summons: pick a bonus Kioku on wish-list banners.
  • Story: the client is ready for a Kazumi Magica story, with Normal, Hard and Nightmare unlocks.
New event type

Scratch cards

not active yet
  • You get cards made of cells. Scratch one cell at a time, or use Scratch all to open every cell on the card at once.
  • Cells line up like a bingo card. The card shows when you are one cell away from a line ("reach") and when a line is complete, and lines give rewards.
  • Some cards are random. The screen counts how many cards you have left to open.
  • The event has its own missions and exchange tickets. A badge on the home screen shows when you can scratch.
Menus

New shop

from a set date

The shop gets a new layout with the tabs Recommended, Sale, Magia Stones and Subscriptions. It switches over at a release time set by the server, so the old shop stays until then.

  • Subscriptions list their bonuses, such as monthly mission rewards and higher drop counts, with what each bonus applies to.
  • Sold-out items stay in the list and are marked as sold out.
Exchange

Kioku ticket exchange

Kioku tickets get their own exchange screen. Its tabs are 2D backgrounds, 3D backgrounds, Music, Models, Limited and Other. You can play a music track before you exchange for it, and each tab has filters.

Summons

Bonus Kioku on wish-list banners

Wish-list banners can come with a bonus Kioku that you choose yourself. Depending on the banner, you confirm the pick for free or pay with summon tickets, Magia Stones or paid Magia Stones. You can change or cancel the pick later, and some banners offer it only until you get the pick-up.

Quality of life

New filters everywhere

The sort and filter pop-ups were rebuilt. You can now filter by series and team (Magia Record teams included), role, element, rarity, enemy type and unlock status. The game saves your choices per list: Kioku, portraits, rental and support Kioku, Crisis, the gallery and the home-screen settings. Link Raid help requests are filtered by category now.

Also in 3.25.0

Story

The client is ready for a Kazumi Magica story map, with Normal, Hard and Nightmare unlocks and its own release time, plus a new series with its own release time.

Step-Up skills

The skill button follows the current Step-Up level and the SP it will cost, also during auto battle. Switched skill buttons go back to normal when their turn is over.

Crisis

The boss info on the Crisis stage screen has a countdown timer.

Score Attack

Story-event Score Attacks follow a schedule and show a "not started yet" message before they open.

Phones

Taps can give vibration feedback, and the game handles split-screen mode.

Simulator status

The simulator now runs on version 3.25.0. The battle code did not change, so every result stays the same as on 3.24.0. New kioku and events from the next data download will be added once it arrives.
