**Client update - 3.25.0**
What the new game version changes in how battles and menus work. Data changes are in the data-update notes.

**⚔️ Battles**
- No rule changes this time. Damage, buffs, conditions and auto-battle work exactly as in 3.24.0.
- Step-Up skills: the skill button follows the current Step-Up level and its SP cost, also during auto battle.

**🎟️ Scratch cards (not active yet)**
- A new event type: scratch the cells of a card one by one, or open the whole card with Scratch all.
- Cells form lines like a bingo card. The card shows when a line is one cell away and when it is complete; lines give rewards.
- Some cards are random. The event has its own missions and exchange tickets, and a home-screen badge when you can scratch.

**🛒 Shop**
- New shop layout with Recommended, Sale, Magia Stones and Subscriptions tabs. It switches over at a release time set by the server.
- Subscriptions list their bonuses (monthly mission rewards, higher drop counts) and what they apply to. Sold-out items stay listed and are marked.

**🎫 Kioku ticket exchange**
- Kioku tickets get their own exchange screen: 2D and 3D home backgrounds, music (with preview), models, limited and other items, each with filters.

**🌟 Summons**
- Wish-list banners can come with a bonus Kioku you pick yourself: free, or for summon tickets, Magia Stones or paid Magia Stones, depending on the banner. The pick can be changed or cancelled.

**🔎 Filters**
- Sort and filter pop-ups were rebuilt: series and team (Magia Record teams included), role, element, rarity, enemy type, unlock status. Choices are saved per list.
- Link Raid help requests are filtered by category.

**🛠️ Also**
- Story: the client is ready for a Kazumi Magica story map with Normal, Hard and Nightmare unlocks.
- Crisis: the boss info on the stage screen has a countdown timer.
- Story-event Score Attacks follow a schedule and show a "not started yet" message before they open.
- Phones: vibration feedback on taps and split-screen support.

**🧮 Simulator**
exedra-dmg-calc runs on 3.25.0. The battle code is unchanged, so results are the same as on 3.24.0.
