**Client update - 3.24.0**
What the new game version changes in how battles and menus work. Data changes are in the data-update notes.

**⚔️ New: Element Drive** (no units yet)
- A levelled mark on an enemy. Only Special Attacks from the unit that placed it level it up, and only if that unit has the mark's element.
- An attacker with the mark's role can add several levels with one Special Attack. Other roles add one level.
- One Drive owner per enemy at a time. The mark stays when its owner dies or leaves the battle.

**💥 New: Drive break boosts** (no units yet)
- Buffs that add break gauge damage (percent or flat) against an enemy carrying your own Drive at exactly the level the buff names.
- A boost can be limited to Battle Skills, Special Attacks or Basic Attacks, or work with all of them. The break gauge shows a Drive effect on boosted hits.

**🔥 New: Over Break** (no units yet)
- A debuff that lets a broken enemy's damage bonus go past its normal cap.
- Works only when the bonus is already capped, the enemy's Drive is at max level and comes from the same unit as the debuff, and the hit is a Special Attack from a unit with the Drive's element.
- The first qualifying Special Attack opens Over Break. Later ones raise the bonus up to a set extra cap. Attackers with the debuff's role add more per hit.
- Ends when the break ends or no active Drive is left on the enemy.

**🛠️ Menus**
- Google Play Games on PC is supported. Its rewards go to the present box, and the camera feature is not available there.
- Achievements are tracked for Steam and Google Play Games.
- New key bindings: move the character forward and back, raise or lower a number. Mouse right click and wheel can be turned on.
- Some stories show a spoiler warning first. Chapter ends can say the story continues. The gallery story list has section headings.
- If a time-limited tower ends while you are in it, you get sent back to Home. The permanent tower can start new seasons and cap the highest floor per season.
