Version 3.24.0: Element Drive and Over Break arrive in the battle code

3.24.0 adds a new break system built around a levelled mark called Element Drive. When it is maxed and the enemy's break bonus is already at its cap, Special Attacks can push the bonus past the cap. No kioku uses it yet: the new data was not out when these notes were written.

In short

  • Element Drive: a mark on an enemy that only its owner's Special Attacks level up.
  • Drive break boosts: buffs that deal extra break gauge damage to an enemy whose Drive is at a set level.
  • Over Break: Special Attacks push a broken enemy's damage bonus above the normal cap.
  • Outside battle: Google Play Games on PC support, new key bindings, story spoiler warnings, tower season handling.
New mechanic

Element Drive

A levelled mark on the enemy

no units yet
  • Element Drive goes on an enemy and has levels, like the older element stack marks. It replaces an older "element break" mark that no unit used.
  • Only Special Attacks level it up, and only the ones from the unit that put it there, when that unit has the mark's element.
  • If the attacker also has the mark's role, one Special Attack can add several levels at once. Any other role adds one level.
  • An enemy carries one Drive owner at a time. Another unit can't put its own Drive on top while the first one is active.
  • The mark stays on the enemy when its owner dies or leaves the battle.

Drive break boosts

no units yet
  • New buffs raise the break gauge damage you deal, as a percentage or a flat amount.
  • They only work against an enemy that carries your own Drive at exactly the level the buff names. One buff can cover a single Drive level, so a kit may come with one boost per level.
  • A boost can be limited to one attack type (Battle Skill, Special Attack or Basic Attack) or work with all three.
  • The break gauge shows a Drive effect when a hit was boosted this way.
New mechanic

Over Break

Going past the break bonus cap

no units yet

Normally a broken enemy's damage bonus grows with every hit until it reaches its cap. Over Break is a debuff that lets the bonus go further. A Special Attack hit adds to it when all of these are true:

  1. The enemy is broken and its break damage bonus is already at the cap.
  2. The enemy's Element Drive is at max level, and the Drive came from the same unit as the Over Break debuff.
  3. The attacker has the Drive's element, and the hit is a Special Attack. The hit that raises the bonus to its cap does not count yet.
  • The first qualifying Special Attack only opens Over Break. The game plays a new effect and sets the extra cap. Later Special Attacks add to the bonus until that cap is reached.
  • How much each hit adds depends on the attacker's role: units with the debuff's role add more than everyone else.
  • The extra bonus is added on top of the capped break bonus for all damage against that enemy while it stays broken.
  • It ends when the break ends (the enemy recovers or uses an HP gauge revive) or when no active Drive is left on the enemy.

Teambuilding: the Drive owner, its Drive break boosts and Over Break all point at one unit's Special Attack. Expect a carry that builds Drive with Special Attacks, partners of the same element to finish the levels, and Special Attack spam once the enemy is broken.

Outside battle

Menus and modes

Google Play Games on PC

The client now supports the PC version of Google Play Games. Play Games rewards go to your present box (they can take a while). The camera feature is not available there.

Achievements

Achievement tracking now runs for both Steam and Google Play Games. It counts things like story chapters read, PvP and Link Raid results, enhance quests and player level.

Key bindings

New keys can move the character forward and back, and increase or decrease a number in quantity pickers. Mouse right click and wheel can be turned on.

Story

Some stories now show a spoiler warning first, and the end of a chapter can tell you that the story continues. The gallery's story list for characters and kioku has section headings.

Towers

If a time-limited tower ends while you are in it, a pop-up sends you back to Home. The permanent tower can start new seasons and can cap how high you can climb in a season.

Simulator

exedra-dmg-calc runs on 3.24.0 and already does Element Drive levels, Drive break boosts and Over Break the way the game does. Kits that use them show up once the new game data is out.
