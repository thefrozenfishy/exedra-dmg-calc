Lilly Candy arrives, and Element Drive finally gets a unit

No client update today (still version 3.24.0). The new game data brings a new character, Lilly Candy, with the first kit built around Element Drive. It also brings the Stellar Photo Op Hunt event with its Score Attack, chapter 2 of Sorana's story, and a new Link Raid season.

In short

  • New kioku: Overdrive Impulse (Lilly Candy, 5★ Void Breaker), from 16 Nov. She uses Web Clusters, Poppin' Candy and Void Drive.
  • Element Drive is live: the break mechanic added in 3.24.0 now has its first user, so you'll see Void Drive levels and Over Break on enemies.
  • Events: Stellar Photo Op Hunt, 16 Nov to 18 Dec. Its Score Attack (Lunar Excavator Witch) runs 16 to 30 Nov. Link Raid season 33 runs 19 Nov to 3 Dec.
  • Stages: Sorana Nagiboshi's Memory, Luna (chapter 2, from 13 Nov), Mirror Layers 121 to 125, a new Ferris Wheel Link Raid EX level, and Lunar Excavator Witch Heartphial quests.
New kioku

Overdrive Impulse

Lilly Candy · 5★ Void Breakerfrom 16 Nov

Lilly Candy is a new character. Her kit runs on three resources: Web Clusters (max 4), the self-buff Poppin' Candy, and the enemy mark Void Drive.

Basic attack

100% void DMG to one enemy, and +1 Web Cluster.

Battle skill: Spinfluence

135% void DMG to all enemies, and applies Void Drive to them (2 turns). Also gives her Poppin' Candy (2 turns) and +1 Web Cluster. While Poppin' Candy is up, her break gauge depletion is higher and her follow-up attack's break bonus is +150%. Each hit also gives all allies +5% special attack DMG and gives her +5% SPD, each stacking up to 4 times.

Special attack: Overdrive Impulse

Up to 280% void DMG to all enemies (Lv 10). All allies deal +20% DMG against elemental weakness for 2 turns, and void allies get a further +5%. With Poppin' Candy active she also gains up to +30% SPD (2 turns) and +1 Web Cluster.

Passive: Assault Chase

  • She starts the battle at 0 Web Clusters, with more break gauge depletion and up to +150% break bonus (Lv 10).
  • Each time she or an ally deals void DMG with a basic attack, battle skill or special attack, she gets 3 MP.
  • Each buff she receives from a battle skill gives her +1 Web Cluster.
  • At 4 Web Clusters she makes a follow-up attack. She also makes one each time an enemy is defeated, and that one is free. It deals 75% void DMG to all enemies, applies Void Drive (2 turns) and gives all allies +5% ATK (2 turns, up to 4 stacks).
  • When an enemy's break bonus hits its cap, she gets 15 MP.

Sub passive: Haste Technique

On a Breaker: +20% SPD and +25% break bonus, and her battle skill raises her break gauge depletion for 2 turns.

EX crys: A Sweet Moment from Me to You!

She starts the battle with 1 Web Cluster. When an enemy is broken, she gets +10% SPD for 1 turn.

Teambuilding: she charges Web Clusters through buffs from battle skills, so pair her with supports whose battle skills buff her. Her special attack boosts damage against elemental weakness for the whole team, and void attackers get the most from her.

Mechanic

Void Drive and Over Break in play

3.24.0 added Element Drive, but until now no unit used it. Lilly Candy applies Void Drive with her battle skill and her follow-up attack.

  • Levels: Void Drive starts at Lv 1 and goes up to Lv 3. Only the special attacks of the unit that applied it level it up. A role match raises it by up to 3 levels at once, so one special attack from Lilly Candy can take it straight to Lv 3.
  • Break: allied void Breakers' special attacks deplete more break gauge on an enemy with Void Drive, and the bonus grows with the Drive's level.
  • Over Break: once the enemy's break bonus is at its cap and its Void Drive is at max level, special attacks push the break bonus beyond the cap, by up to a further 100%. It is lost when the break ends or the Drive runs out.

Void Drive lasts 2 turns. Apply it with her battle skill, level it with her special attack, then use your void Breakers' special attacks while the enemy is broken.
