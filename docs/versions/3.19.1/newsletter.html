Madoka Exedra · version dispatch · 2 Oct 2026

3.19.1: same battle engine, a new Kioku and a rose garden

The 27 September patch (Steam files dated 2026-09-27 14:00 UTC) rebuilt the client without touching game logic. Everything new arrived as server master data. The simulator learned five rules along the way.

Compared: unpackedExedra 3.19.0 → 3.19.1 · global-metadata v31 · base_data 4ea7254^ → HEAD

CODE IDENTICAL

GameAssembly is byte-for-byte the same code. The .text, il2cpp, .data and .pdata sections match exactly. Only the build timestamp, the PDB age and the protector's _RDATA changed, and in global-metadata the single string literal "3.19.0" became "3.19.1". Every RVA in the knowledge base and the whole 3.19.0 decompile stay valid.

What the simulator learned

Checking the new data against the engine turned up one new type and four older rules that the new content leans on. All five are ported from the 3.19 decompile and covered by a new engine check. They are on branch claude/version-3.19.1, not pushed.

Magic Seal (LOCK_SPECIAL_ATTACK)

A sealed unit can't use its ultimate and keeps its EP until the seal ends. It only lands on EP-based units (never BP units like Metallicized Projectile, never enemies). Enemy AI aims it at unsealed targets, preferring the main target, then broken units, then the highest ATK.

CanAddTo 0x16d2940 · UnitCondition.Refresh 0x15c9b00 · ValidateCanExecuteAct 0x17e27a0 · AI 0x16d29d0 / 0x16d2c00

"Number of broken enemies" conditions

Contents 206/207 (units with break bonus ≥ or < X) and 22 (break bonus at max) always returned false. About 190 kit and passive rows use them, for example ATK or SPD per broken enemy. They now count, and a unit's break bonus starts at 0 instead of 100%, as in the game. That also fixes "own break bonus ≥ 80%", which used to be true for every unbroken unit.

team checker 0x17e5f30 cases 5/6 · unit checker 0x17e3f10 case 0x16 · BattleUnit ctor 0x1389650

Notice team counts (313–316)

These count "allies hit by this skill", "broken units hit", and "removable buffs/debuffs just applied". The code marked them as not handled by the game, but the game does handle them. The Solo Raid party buff's Vanguard points (313) and the new Rose Garden Witch Magic gain on debuffs (316) depend on them.

0x17e5f30 cases 0x139–0x13c · AddStateInfo flags in StateAbilityEffect.Triggering 0x1901cb0

Instant DOT burst (IMM_SLIP_DMG)

This was implemented as "DOT immunity" and did nothing. It actually deals every DOT on the target × its remaining turns at once and removes them. It affects the Nightmare Stinger, My Creations and Marigold Dadaism ultimates and enemies in 36 stages.
